She knew she couldn't continue to struggle academically, but she also couldn't imagine giving up on her music. With a newfound sense of determination, Jessica vowed to find a way to balance her passions and her studies, and to make the most of her time at Ontario University.[SEP]Growing up in a small town in Ontario, Jessica's love for music began at a young age.
