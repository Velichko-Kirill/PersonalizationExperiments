[SEP]Mark Thompson was born on July 13, 1972, in Vancouver, Canada. He grew up in a loving family with parents who instilled in him the importance of education and environmental responsibility. His parents, John and Sarah, were high school sweethearts who had met while volunteering at a local park cleanup event. They shared a deep love for nature and their community, which they passed on to Mark.

As a child, Mark was a curious and adventurous boy who spent hours exploring the woods behind his house, collecting leaves and rocks, and watching the birds fly overhead. He was especially close to his mother, who would take him on long walks through the nearby park, teaching him about the different plant species and the importance of conservation.

Mark's interest in science and mathematics began when he started attending the Vancouver Technical School. His teachers encouraged his curiosity, and he excelled in subjects like biology and chemistry. It was during this time that Mark developed a passion for environmental conservation, which would later become the driving force behind his career choices.

As Mark entered high school, he became increasingly aware of the world's environmental problems. He began to read about climate change, deforestation, and pollution, feeling an overwhelming sense of responsibility to do something. His parents, sensing his concern, encouraged him to pursue a degree in Environmental Science at the University of British Columbia.

During his time at university, Mark threw himself into various eco-activism groups and volunteering projects. He became an active member of the student body, organizing protests and rallies against pollution and advocating for sustainable development practices. His peers admired his dedication and passion, and he quickly gained a reputation as one of the most driven students in his class.

After graduating with honors from the University of British Columbia, Mark landed a job as a senior consultant at AECOM, a leading environmental consulting firm. He quickly made a name for himself within the company, known for his expertise in sustainable development and community engagement. Over the years, he collaborated on numerous high-profile projects, including the redevelopment of Vancouver's waterfront area.

Mark's personal life is marked by a strong sense of independence and self-reliance. Despite having an extensive social network, he has difficulty forming intimate relationships. His closest friends are scattered across the country, and he prefers to maintain a low profile in his personal life. This aloofness stems from unresolved childhood fears of abandonment, which have led him to prioritize his emotional safety over genuine connections with others.

In 2005, Mark met Emma, a fellow environmentalist who shared his passion for conservation. They started dating casually but never formalized their relationship. Emma eventually moved away to pursue a career opportunity, leaving Mark feeling relieved and yet unfulfilled.

Throughout his life, Mark has maintained a modest lifestyle, residing in a small apartment on Robson Street in Vancouver's downtown area. He enjoys spending time alone, exploring the city's hidden gems, and engaging in solo activities like hiking and reading.

In 2010, Mark received the Environmental Excellence Award from the City of Vancouver for his outstanding contributions to sustainable development and community engagement. This recognition further solidified his reputation as a leading expert in his field, but it also brought him more attention and pressure to perform.

Mark's introspective nature often leads him to question his choices and priorities. He struggles with the fear of abandonment and rejection that has defined his life since childhood, making genuine connections with others feel like a constant risk. Despite this, he remains committed to his work and values, using them as a guiding force in his life.

Today, Mark Thompson continues to work at AECOM, where he is respected by his peers for his expertise and dedication. He continues to navigate the complexities of his personal relationships, always prioritizing his emotional safety while striving for connection and understanding.
